# VIREZIA — Value Propositions

**Version:** 2.0 (May 2026)
**Status:** Canonical
**Supersedes:** v1.0 (DD-pricing-front formula)

---

## Architectural Note

VIREZIA serves **three audiences** with three distinct surfaces. Each one has its own value proposition. Do not mix.

| Audience | Surface | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer / Investor | Homepage, Featured properties, Apply | Editorial, curatorial, invitational |
| Asset Owner / Developer | `/featured` (formerly `/partners`), submission flow | Selective, professional, demanding |
| VIREZIA Circle | Single line, reference only | Restrained, undisclosed |

---

## TRACK 1 — Buyer / Investor (Public Front)

### Core proposition

> VIREZIA is a private market for distinctive properties — selected personally, presented with context, and offered by application.
> Each featured property is curated for its design, architecture, location, story, or investment character. Off the public market.

### Voice formula (homepage, Apply, featured property pages)

The buyer doesn't come to VIREZIA to *find* a property in a search field.
They come because they want to be **introduced** to properties they wouldn't have found alone — by someone whose selection they trust.

### What to emphasize (front)

- **Curation as the product.** "We feature what we would consider ourselves."
- **Off-market positioning.** Most featured properties are not listed publicly elsewhere.
- **Context over spec sheet.** Architecture, location, story, investment thesis — whichever fits.
- **Access by application.** "By invitation. By application. By referral."
- **Distinct character.** Each property featured because of *something specific* that sets it apart.

### What never goes on this surface

- Due diligence pricing or packages
- Verification mechanics
- Market intelligence claims as a public product
- Service-catalog process tables (steps 1-2-3)
- Generic differentiator lists

### Sample copy patterns

**Homepage hero (LOCKED — May 2026):**
> Curated real estate, selected — not listed.
> A network where international investors meet and find good opportunities.

**Featured property opening (architecturally led — Las Orcas):**
> Las Orcas. A coastal retreat designed by Robert Couturier — a name that needs no introduction in the world of design.
> Eleven residences. One architect. One stretch of Pacific coastline.

**Featured property opening (investment-led — Árbol de Piedra):**
> Árbol de Piedra. 36.7 hectares in Argentina, off the public market.
> A defined investment thesis, structured for a specific buyer profile.

**Apply page:**
> Tell us what you're looking for. Create your VIREZIA profile.
> We work with international investors, developers, and asset owners — and respond personally within 48 hours.

## TRACK 2 — Asset Owner / Developer (`/featured` only)

### Core proposition

> VIREZIA features a small number of exceptional properties each year. Owners and developers who join the platform receive editorial repositioning, curated audience access, and placement in our network of vetted buyers.

### Voice formula

This is **not** a "list with us" pitch. It is a **gatekeeping conversation**.

The owner approaches VIREZIA. The owner pays for repositioning. The owner is then evaluated for inclusion. Not the other way around.

### What to emphasize (front)

- **Selectivity.** "We feature a limited number per year."
- **Editorial repositioning as the service.** Each property gets a story, voice, narrative, audience match.
- **Distribution to qualified demand.** Curated buyer base, REACH network, campaign infrastructure.
- **Status of being featured.** Inclusion is itself the value.

### What stays in backend (post-qualification)

- Specific repositioning fees and retainers
- Campaign budget structures
- Internal intelligence and audience data
- Cross-selling mechanics within VIREZIA Circle

### Standard formula (for `/featured` page)

> VIREZIA features a limited number of exceptional properties each year — sourced globally, with current focus across Latin America.
> If your property has architectural distinction, design lineage, location character, or story we can tell — we'd like to hear from you.
> Submission begins with a brief intake. Featured selection is reviewed quarterly.

### What we look for (filter, not pitch)

- Exceptional architecture, design, or location
- Story we can build editorial around
- Owner willing to support repositioning of the offer
- Pricing aligned with market reality (not aspirational listing prices)
- Clean legal title or clear regularization path

## TRACK 3 — VIREZIA Circle (Invitation Only)

### Public communication rule

**One sentence only on public pages:**

> VIREZIA Circle — by invitation only.

That is the complete public description.

### What is never publicly disclosed

- What Circle contains
- Membership criteria
- Deal flow mechanics
- Pre-market access structure
- Internal cross-selling
- Member benefits

### Why this restriction exists

The Circle's value is partially in its opacity. Describing membership erodes membership. Public pages may signal that Circle exists; they may not explain it.

If the visitor is qualified to be in Circle, they will know to apply or be referred. Anyone else doesn't need the explanation.

## Cross-Track Differentiators (Optional — for press, partnerships, internal)

These are not for the public-facing site. They are for contexts where VIREZIA needs to explain itself externally (interviews, partnership decks, content positioning).

1. **Curator-led, not algorithm-led.** Each property selected personally. Editorial, not data-driven.
2. **Front editorial, backend intelligence.** Visible: story. Invisible: verification, sourcing, audience-matching.
3. **Two-sided model with one funnel.** Asset owners pay for visibility. Buyers pay for access. Each campaign compounds the other side.
4. **Selectivity as a competitive moat.** Cannot be copied by scaling. Requires taste, network, and curatorial restraint.
5. **Global scope, Latin American depth.** Currently strongest where Lucas is on the ground. Welcoming exceptional properties worldwide.
